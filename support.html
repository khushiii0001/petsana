Petsana Support

Medication reminders, daily check-ins and vet-ready reports for pets who need extra care.

Need help? Email petsana.help@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days.

I'm not getting dose reminders

Check Settings › Notifications › Petsana and make sure notifications are allowed. Open Petsana at least once a week: reminders are planned two weeks ahead each time you open it. Reminders need an active subscription or free trial.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

On your iPhone, go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Petsana › Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged. Your records stay viewable and exportable after cancelling.

I got a new phone. How do I restore my subscription?

Open Petsana › More › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I share records with my vet?

Go to Insights › Create vet report, choose the dates, then tap Share to email or AirDrop the PDF.

I'm going away. How do I brief my pet sitter?

Open your pet, tap ••• › Care sheet for a sitter. It includes medicines, emergency contacts and a tick-box dose checklist.

How do I export all my data?

More › Export all data creates spreadsheet files you can open in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets. This is always free.
