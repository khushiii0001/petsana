Petsana Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Petsana helps you keep track of your pet's medications, health observations and records. We built it so that your information stays with you. This policy explains what Petsana does with your data, in plain language.

The short version

  • Everything you enter is stored on your device.
  • We do not collect, see, sell or share your data. We have no servers that receive it.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no third-party analytics.

Information you enter

Pet profiles, medications, dose logs, check-ins, condition logs, weights, vaccines, vet visits, photos and scanned documents are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync (when available), this data is stored in your own private iCloud account, managed by Apple; we cannot access it.

Camera, photos and scanned documents

Petsana uses the camera and photo library only when you choose to add a photo or scan a document. Text in scanned documents is read on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition and is never uploaded.

Notifications

Medication and vaccine reminders are scheduled on your device. No reminder content is sent to us.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS. Petsana never receives your face data or passcode.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple shares only anonymous sales information with us. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Reports and exports you share

When you create a vet report, care sheet or data export and share it (for example by email or AirDrop), it goes only where you send it. Once shared, the recipient's handling of it is outside our control.

Deleting your data

Delete a pet in the app to remove all of its records, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. If you use iCloud sync, you can also remove Petsana data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Children

Petsana is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Veterinary advice

Petsana is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide veterinary advice, diagnoses or treatment recommendations.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
